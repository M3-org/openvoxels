Preserve the Metaverse. Empower its Creators.

Openvoxels is a community-led effort to preserve, curate, augment, and promote historical digital artifacts from virtual worlds—starting with Voxels (formerly Cryptovoxels). We archive worlds, upgrade datasets to open formats like glTF and VRM, decentralize storage, and build living museums so culture endures.

Open standards: glTF 2.0 • VRM • JSON Decentralized storage: Arweave • IPFS • Filecoin

Our Mission

Create durable, interoperable records of virtual worlds. We snapshot cities, convert proprietary formats to open ones, enrich assets with provenance metadata, and publish them to decentralized storage. Then we bring these artifacts to life via public, immersive “living museums.”

Preservation Interoperability Creator Empowerment Open Research

Why File > App?

Apps are ephemeral. Files endure. By embracing open formats like glTF and VRM, anyone can read, remix, and migrate assets across engines and platforms—now and decades from now. That’s how we align the metaverse with user ownership and right-of-exit.

Upgraded File Formats

Decentralized Storage

Mirror assets to Arweave/IPFS for permanence. Replace centralized URLs in metadata with permaweb links; add HTML previews with AR/VR.

Smaller, Faster, Open
