Our Mission

Openvoxels is a data DAO and DeSci art project focused on preserving the cultural heritage of virtual worlds. We started with Cryptovoxels/Voxels, one of the first blockchain-based virtual worlds.

"File > App - If you want to create digital artifacts that last, they must be files you can control, in formats that are easy to retrieve and read."

We believe digital ownership means more than just having an NFT - it means having actual control over your digital creations in formats that will outlast any single platform.

🌐

Key Projects

Wearables Archive

Converted 34,000+ Cryptovoxels wearables from proprietary .vox format to glTF, uploaded to Arweave for permanent storage, and created VRM avatars for cross-platform use.

Virtual World Snapshots

Regular 3D snapshots of entire virtual worlds, creating a "Wayback Machine" for the metaverse. Exported to multiple formats and platforms including VRChat and Hyperfy.

Living Museums

Immersive galleries showcasing the evolution of virtual worlds, featuring historical builds, avatar costumes, and cultural artifacts from the early metaverse era.

Smooth Voxels Tools

CLI tools for converting between voxel formats, enabling creators to work with accessible voxel editors while outputting to modern 3D standards.
