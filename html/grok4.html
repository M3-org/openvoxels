About Openvoxels

Openvoxels is a DAO dedicated to preserving, curating, augmenting, and promoting historical digital artifacts from the metaverse, starting with Voxels (formerly Cryptovoxels). We believe virtual worlds are ephemeral, and without proper archiving, their cultures and creations risk being lost. Our mission: Leverage interoperable open standards to preserve digital artifacts, pave the way for metaverse alignment, empower users, and build living museums that tell the story of how the metaverse was built.

From snapshots of Origin City to immortalizing voxel wearables, we ensure these virtual treasures stand the test of time. Join us in fighting for digital preservation and true ownership in the open metaverse.

Key Projects & Datasets

  • Origin City Archives: 3D snapshots of Voxels districts from 2019-2024, exported to glTF for preservation and remixing. Explore on Sketchfab.
  • Voxel Wearables Dataset: Snapshot of 34,000+ wearables converted to glTF/SVOX, with upgraded metadata and decentralized storage on Arweave. View on GitHub.
  • Wearables Museum: A digital museum showcasing Voxels history, with AR/VR previews, VRM avatars, and immersive experiences. Visit in Hyperfy.
  • Interop Tools: Smooth Voxels CLI for format conversion, VRM assembly for avatars, and more. GitHub Repo.
  • Full World Snapshots: Backups to GitHub, Arweave, and Sketchfab for longevity. Latest Snapshot.

Our work drives incentives for open formats like glTF and VRM, enabling remix culture and metaverse interoperability.

Recent Updates (Summer 2024)

In Summer 2024, we released Smooth Voxels 2.3.0, launched a Wearables Art Contest (winners showcased interoperability), upgraded wearables metadata to decentralized storage, and optimized VRM avatars for platforms like Oncyber and Hyperfy. We also fixed Voxels VRM sprites and explored on-chain wearables.

Key milestone: A blockchain snapshot of 34,000+ wearables, with plans for VRM passports and a new protocol-inspired virtual world builder.

Why Voxels?

Voxels (formerly Cryptovoxels) is the "Cryptopunks of virtual worlds"—the first to integrate smart contracts with live virtual spaces in 2018. It pioneered affordable land, 3D NFT wearables, and a vibrant cryptoart scene. Voxel art is ultra-accessible, making it a perfect lab for interoperability experiments. We focus on Voxels to prove permissionless innovation, spread its culture, and ensure its history endures. Niche is powerful—it's where true creativity thrives.
