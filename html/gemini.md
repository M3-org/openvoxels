## The Problem: Our Culture is Built on Disappearing Ink

Virtual worlds are more than just games; they are cultural landmarks. But what happens when the platform changes, pivots, or shuts down?

![Broken links and missing content plaguing a virtual world](https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/3b4638e4c548496b9706573efe8f1f6d93be65e1e03f3b2089c1355cae623d4b.jpg)
*(In June 2024, a centralized server issue caused assets across Voxels to break, highlighting the fragility of our digital heritage.)*

Centralized servers, proprietary file formats, and platform lock-in put our creations at risk. Our history, art, and identities can vanish overnight. We believe in a different path.

> ### **File > App**
>
> "All software is ephemeral... If you want to create digital artifacts that last, they must be files you can control, in formats that are easy to retrieve and read." - *[post by kepano](https://stephango.com/file-over-app)*

## Our Solution: From Locked Apps to Lasting Files

We are undertaking a massive digital preservation effort, starting with the historically significant world of **Voxels**. We see it as the Florence of the cryptoart renaissance, and we are its dedicated archivists.

*   **Archival Snapshots:** We take full, high-fidelity 3D snapshots of the entire Voxels world, preserving builds and culture at a specific moment in time.
*   **Open Standards:** We convert proprietary `.vox` models into open, interoperable formats like **glTF** and portable **VRM** avatars that can travel across the metaverse.
*   **Decentralized Storage:** We rescue assets from centralized servers and upload them to permanent storage on **Arweave**, ensuring they can't be taken down.
*   **Upgraded Metadata:** We enhance NFT metadata with new, high-quality thumbnails, AR/VR previews, and permanent links, giving true longevity and value to collectors.

## Key Projects & Initiatives

### 🏛️ The Wearables Museum

We have archived and upgraded over **34,000 unique Voxel wearables**. Every single one has been:
*   Converted to **glTF** format with embedded metadata.
*   Given new, optimized GIF thumbnails and interactive AR/VR previews.
*   Permanently archived on Arweave.

This is a foundational piece of metaverse history, now preserved for generations.

### 👤 Interoperable Avatars & Passports

Your identity shouldn't be locked to one world. We've developed a pipeline to convert any Voxel costume into a portable **VRM avatar**.
*   Generated VRMs for all parcel holders and supporters.
*   Created sprite-sheets for performance-friendly avatar imposters.
*   Prototyping **"Voxels Passports,"** unique 3D NFTs that act as a digital identity card for your avatar.
